Breach Point

The arena is held and you are the only thing clearing it. Your carbine fires, reloads and runs dry; the hostiles across the floor move, spot you through the gaps and shoot back. Clear the round before your health or the clock gives out, and the next one starts over. Headshots pay triple. Firing on the move opens your cone of fire, so stopping, crouching and aiming down the sights is how a fight is won.
